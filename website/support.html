General

Is KatharScan really free?

Yes. Unlimited scanning, unlimited on-device OCR in Latin script, no watermark, no account, no ads, and all five export formats are free forever. Pro adds optional power tools — filters, signatures, password-protected PDFs, batch export, Device Migration, and extra OCR languages — but nothing free ever moves behind a paywall later.

Do I need to create an account?

No. KatharScan has no account system at all. Your documents live only on your device.

Does KatharScan work offline?

Scanning, OCR, organizing, and exporting all work fully offline — everything runs on your device. An internet connection is only used to validate a Pro purchase with Apple or Google.

Scanning & OCR

The scanner says my device isn't supported. What now?

A small number of older or lower-memory devices don't support the advanced document scanner. When that happens, KatharScan automatically offers a manual alternative: take a photo with your regular Camera app, then use KatharScan's Import option to crop and save it — you get the same result with one extra tap.

Why is OCR not finding any text?

Make sure the page is well-lit and the text is in focus. If you're scanning a language other than Latin script (English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, and similar), you'll need a Pro OCR language pack — check Settings → OCR language to see which one is active.

Which OCR languages are supported?

Latin script is free and bundled. Pro adds Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and Devanagari (Hindi). Arabic and Hebrew are available as app interface languages but aren't currently supported as OCR recognition languages.

Pro & billing

How do I restore a Pro purchase on a new device?

Open Settings → Restore Purchases while signed into the same Apple ID or Google account you originally subscribed with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed entirely through the App Store or Google Play — KatharScan can't cancel it for you. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Play Store → Menu → Subscriptions.

Moving to a new device

Does KatharScan sync between my devices?

No — by design. Device Migration (Pro) is a manual, one-time export/import: you create a single encrypted backup file, move it to your new device yourself, and import it there. It's a deliberate one-time transfer, not background sync.

What's next

Will you add Dropbox or OneDrive support?

Both are on the roadmap for a future release, alongside improved DOCX formatting that preserves more of a page's original layout. Sharing to Drive, iCloud, and anywhere else already works today through your device's own share sheet.
