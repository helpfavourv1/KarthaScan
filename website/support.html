General

Is KatharScan really free?

Yes. Unlimited scanning, unlimited OCR, no watermark, no account, and all 5 export formats are free forever. The only optional purchase is "Remove Ads" — a one-time payment that removes all ads.

Do I need to create an account?

No. KatharScan has no account system. Your documents live only on your device.

Does KatharScan work offline?

Scanning, OCR, organizing, and exporting all work fully offline. An internet connection is only used to validate a purchase or serve ads.

OCR

Which OCR languages are supported?

All OCR languages are free: Latin, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and Devanagari (Hindi).

Why is OCR not finding any text?

Make sure the page is well-lit and the text is in focus.

Ads & Purchases

How do I remove ads?

Go to Settings → Remove Ads and make a one-time purchase. Ads are removed forever.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Open Settings → Restore Purchases while signed into the same Apple ID or Google account.
