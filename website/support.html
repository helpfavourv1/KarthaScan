General

Is KatharScan really free?

Yes. Unlimited scanning, unlimited OCR, no watermark, no account, and every export format is free forever. The only optional purchase is "Remove Ads" — a one-time payment that removes all ads.

Do I need to create an account?

No. KatharScan has no account system. Your documents live only on your device.

Does KatharScan work offline?

Scanning, organizing, exporting, and Latin-script OCR work fully offline — no internet connection at all. Chinese, Korean, and Japanese recognition is offered only where your device's Google Play services can deliver the models, and the app labels those scripts unavailable otherwise. An internet connection is otherwise only used to validate a purchase or serve ads.

OCR

Which OCR languages are supported?

OCR runs on-device. Latin script is built in and covers English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, and many more languages with no download. Chinese, Korean, and Japanese are offered only where your device's Google Play services can deliver the models; where they cannot, the app shows those scripts as unsupported.

Why is OCR not finding any text?

Make sure the page is well-lit and the text is in focus.

Ads & Purchases

How do I remove ads?

Go to Settings → Remove Ads and make a one-time purchase. Ads are removed forever.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Open Settings → Restore Purchases while signed into the same Apple ID or Google account.
