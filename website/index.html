Everything between the paper and the finished file

One app covers the whole job: capture, text recognition, editing, converting and filing.

Scan with a clean result

The camera scanner finds the edges of the page, straightens it and cleans up the image. Scan one page or a whole stack, or import photos and PDFs you already have.

Text recognition (OCR)

Every scan is read and becomes searchable. Select a region of a page to pull out just that text, copy it, or save it with the document. Recognition runs on your phone.

Many scripts, one app

Latin, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Hindi and Marathi (Devanagari), Arabic, Russian, Ukrainian, Greek, Hebrew, Thai and Bengali. Pages in other scripts are picked up automatically when their language pack is installed.

Edit and sign

Draw and annotate, add your signature, place text, notes, dates, checkboxes and seals, fill in forms, or add a watermark. Every edit sits on the page and can be moved, changed or undone.

Fix the page itself

Crop, rotate, resize, erase marks, and apply grayscale, black and white, color enhance or shadow removal filters. Revert any page to the original in one tap.

Manage pages

Reorder, duplicate, delete, insert or replace pages, rotate them, or split selected pages into a new document. Edits stay with their page when you rearrange.

Export in six formats

PDF, Word, plain text, JPG, PNG and CSV. Choose A4 or Letter, pick a quality level, or set a target file size so the result fits an upload limit.

Convert files

Turn Word, text and CSV files into PDF, split a PDF into images, or change an image into another format. Complex scripts and Chinese, Japanese and Korean text are kept intact in the PDF.

ID card mode

Scan the front and back of a card and get both sides on one PDF page, ready to print or send.

Folders, tags and search

File scans in folders, add tags, mark favorites, and find any document by the words inside it or by its tags.

Print, share and back up

Print or email a document exactly as edited. Move everything to a new phone with an encrypted backup you protect with a password.

Private by design

Your documents are stored on your phone. There is no account to create and no cloud to upload to; a file leaves the device only when you share it.

How it works

  1. ScanCapture pages with the camera or import images and PDFs.
  2. EditCrop, filter, annotate, sign and arrange the pages.
  3. ExportSave as PDF, Word, text, JPG, PNG or CSV, or print and share.
