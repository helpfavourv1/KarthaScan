Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

No server. No cloud. No ads. Your documents stay on your device. Camera access is for scanning only.

That sentence is the whole policy in spirit. Here's the detail behind it.

What KatharScan accesses, and why

  • Camera — to scan documents. Every image is processed on your device and stored locally. KatharScan never uploads a photo anywhere to perform a scan or run OCR.
  • Photo library — only if you choose to import an existing photo instead of using the camera. KatharScan doesn't browse your library on its own; you pick the photo.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your location, contacts, call log, SMS messages, or an advertising identifier. There's no ad SDK in this app at all, and there never will be — see our Trust Promise.

Where your documents live

Scanned pages, extracted text, folders, and tags are stored only in this app's local storage on your device. KatharScan has no backend server — there's nothing for us to store, and nothing for us to lose in a breach, because we never receive it in the first place.

OCR (text recognition)

Text recognition runs entirely on-device, using Apple's and Google's own on-device machine learning engines. No image or extracted text is sent to KatharScan or to any third party to perform OCR.

Sharing a document

When you tap Share, KatharScan hands the exported file to your device's own share sheet — the same system menu every app uses. If you choose Google Drive, iCloud, email, or anywhere else from that menu, the file goes directly from your device to that destination through the operating system. KatharScan itself never sees or touches that transfer, and never talks to Google's or Apple's cloud services on its own.

Device Migration

The Device Migration feature (Pro) lets you export an encrypted backup file and import it on a new device. This is a manual, one-time action you control — never automatic background sync — and the backup file is encrypted with a password only you know before it ever leaves the app.

Purchases

Pro subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play Billing. KatharScan receives only the information needed to confirm your subscription status — never your payment details, which Apple and Google keep on their own systems.

Children's privacy

KatharScan is a general-purpose productivity tool not directed at children, and it doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age — see "What we don't collect" above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the update.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@katharscan.[YOUR_DOMAIN].
