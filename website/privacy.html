Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Your documents and personal files never leave your device. Your documents stay on your device. Camera access is for scanning only.

What KatharScan accesses, and why

  • Camera — to scan documents. Every image is processed on your device and stored locally.
  • Photo library — only if you choose to import an existing photo.
  • Advertising ID — used by Google AdMob to serve ads. You can remove all ads with a one-time in-app purchase.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your location, contacts, call log, SMS messages, or any personal information. We never see your documents.

Where your documents live

Scanned pages, extracted text, folders, and tags are stored only in this app's local storage on your device.

Advertising

KatharScan uses Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial ads. These ads are served by Google and may use your advertising identifier to personalize ads. You can remove all ads forever with a one-time in-app purchase.

Purchases

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play Billing. KatharScan never sees your payment details.

Children's privacy

KatharScan is a general-purpose productivity tool not directed at children, and it doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at accessmakr@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to accessmakr@gmail.com.
